# BriefCheck — proposed scope

Version 3 scope update, authorized 2026-10-02: optional/conflicting source information, editable/custom scope, unanswered counts, 20 local projects, JSON import, retained edits with reconfirmation, revision comparison and client HTML. This supersedes the original one-draft POC/later boundary below. No cloud account, client signature or model inference added.

Agent-proposed candidate, developed under the user's explicit instruction to complete work before approval. No intermediate learner approval is claimed.

## The Unique Kernel
Turn vague freelance client requests into an editable acceptance checklist that keeps each finding attached to the original text and separates suggested criteria from user-confirmed ones.

## Who It's For
A solo freelancer receiving an informal website, video, design or automation request, who needs to ask concrete questions before committing to delivery.

## The Core Loop
Paste the brief → identify gaps and source-backed reminders → edit criteria and record client answers → confirm agreed deliverables → export and continue the conversation.

## Inspiration & Identity
Agent-proposed calm working document: off-white background, forest green actions, editorial serif headings, compact plain-language labels. No reference images or pre-existing frontend are used.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Personal relevance is unestablished. The user's stated goal is to receive a completed hackathon candidate for approval.

## What "Working" Looks Like
The vague coffee-shop sample leaves budget and deadline unknown, flags unlimited revisions, and shows the exact triggering text. The user edits an observable acceptance standard, confirms it, refreshes and exports a document containing that edit, source and confirmation state.

## The POC Boundary
Single browser workspace, English/Chinese, common phrase rules, one local draft, editable criteria and answers, MD/JSON export, browser printing. A local server is optional.

## Later
User-authored additional deliverables, importing project files, versioned client agreements and optional model-assisted suggestions could be investigated after actual user feedback.

## Explicitly Cut
Accounts, cloud sync, paid APIs, automated negotiation, financial advice, automatic client agreement and invented dates or prices. These do not help prove this kernel and require additional trust or infrastructure.
